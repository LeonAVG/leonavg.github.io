Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kawaii Rush ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

In Short: We do not require account registration or collect personal names, but our integrated advertising and analytics partners process technical identifiers.

The Application does not require personal registration. However, to provide free gameplay with ads and analyze performance, third-party services integrated into the app may automatically collect the following information:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) for ad personalization and frequency capping.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and system language settings.
  • Coarse Location Data: General geographic location inferred from IP address (country and city level).
  • Diagnostic and Performance Data: Crash traces, app load times, and in-game session statistics.

2. Third-Party Partners

We work with trusted third-party advertising networks and analytics platforms to support game maintenance and monetization. You can review their respective privacy practices via the links below:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Crashlytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

  • Mintegral: https://www.mintegral.com/en/privacy
  • Bigo Ads: https://www.bigossp.com/privacy
  • Start.io: https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/

3. Data Deletion & User Rights

Under applicable regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to request access to or deletion of technical data collected by our partners, or to reset your Advertising ID directly via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID).

For any data deletion inquiries or requests, please contact us directly via email.

4. Children's Privacy

The Application is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction.
